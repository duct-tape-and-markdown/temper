# Capture friction or structural debt

Two channels, one skill. Check the target first — never re-file a capture
that is already there.

**The bar: exceptional, never a duty.** Most ticks and sessions file nothing.
A capture names something you actually hit or touched this tick, with its
actual cost — never speculative, never a substitute for finishing the job.

## Friction — `.flume/friction/<phase>-<slug>.md`

A pitfall the harness could have warned about, a disproportionately slow
process, missing operational knowledge, or a gate firing on a false positive
— the test is recurrence × cost: something the next tick would likely hit
too. Three parts: `## Symptom`, `## Cost this tick`, `## Suggested fix`. A
fix you can ground in a named invariant may carry an apply-ready diff. The
channel is gitignored; the inbox slice routes each note.

## Structural debt — a build note, `.flume/plan/notes/<TAG>.md`

A second implementation of one job, a hand-roll a sanctioned crate covers,
scaffolding copied once more (the engineering playbook, "One job, one home") — debt you
touched but could not fix in your entry's scope. Consolidation inside your
own entry's surface needs no note. A build tick writes only the note its own
tag names; outside a build tick, the same finding is an inbox record,
`.flume/inbox/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<slug>.md`.
