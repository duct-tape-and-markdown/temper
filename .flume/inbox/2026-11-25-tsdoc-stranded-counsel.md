# TSDoc that states a rule is guidance stranded in a human channel

Intent invariant 8 (amended 2026-11-25), and 0045's move ("leaving the notes in doc comments … invisible to the authoring agent"), applied to the SDK's own API. Census `sdk/src/**` TSDoc for sentences that are rules, not descriptions: "must", "never", "prefer", "use X, not Y". Each goes to its delivered home: kind or field guidance, a clause's guidance, an emit or SDK refusal message, or the `.temper/**` counsel rule. The TSDoc keeps the description. A fold: pins only, net-neutral or negative.
